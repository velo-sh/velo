> **Authority**: OSS Manager / Legal
> **Status**: **IMMUTABLE**

## 1. Licensing

**Constraint**: "Zero Infection."

*   **Permissive**: MIT / Apache 2.0 dual license preferred.
*   **Check**: Use `cargo-deny` to block GPL/AGPL dependencies.

**Constraint**: "Low Friction, High Bar."

*   **DCO**: Developer Certificate of Origin (Signed-off-by) required.
*   **Templates**: Issue and PR templates must be strictly followed.
*   **Code of Conduct**: Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforced.
