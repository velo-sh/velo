> **Status**: Active

---

## Philosophy: Wooden Bucket Theory 🪣

Each version must be a **complete, usable product**. We deliver value incrementally:
- High impact → Low impact
- Easy → Hard
- Fast delivery cycles (1-2 weeks per release)
